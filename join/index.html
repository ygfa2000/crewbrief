Get the iPhone app

The iPhone app is shared through TestFlight, Apple’s free app for trying apps before they are on the App Store. It works like a normal app, with the home screen widget and Apple Watch.

  1. Send your Apple ID email and name below.
  2. When you are added, Apple emails you an invite from TestFlight (usually within a day or two).
  3. Install TestFlight from the App Store.
  4. On your iPhone, open the email, tap View in TestFlight, then Install.
  5. Updates come through TestFlight. Each build works for 90 days, and a new one comes before that.

Request an invite

Use the email of the Apple ID on your iPhone (Settings › your name).
